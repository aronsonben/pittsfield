📜 OUR HISTORY
  • 1752 — Settled as "Pontoosuck"; incorporated 1761 and renamed for British PM William Pitt the Elder. Became a city in 1891.
  • 1791 — A Pittsfield bylaw banning baseball near the new meetinghouse windows is the earliest known written reference to baseball in America. You're welcome, Cooperstown.
  • 1850s — Herman Melville writes Moby-Dick at his farm Arrowhead, staring at the whale-shaped ridge of Mount Greylock. Nathaniel Hawthorne lived nearby; the two famously hiked and argued about whales.
  • 1903–1987 — General Electric transforms Pittsfield into a company town, building power transformers and (in wartime) ordnance. The Housatonic River cleanup from that era continues today.
  • Today — A reinvented city: arts on North Street, the Colonial and Barrington Stage, and Greylock looming over it all like it always has.
🎭 ARTS & CULTURE
  • 🖼️ Berkshire Museum — art, natural history & an aquarium, downtown since 1903.
  • 🎭 Barrington Stage Company & the historic Colonial Theatre — where The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee was born.
  • 🥁 Third Thursdays — North Street shuts down for a free street festival, May through September.
  • 🏡 Arrowhead (Melville's home) and nearby Hancock Shaker Village.
  • 🎼 Tanglewood (Boston Symphony's summer home) is ten minutes down the road in Lenox.
  • 🌲 Onota & Pontoosuc lakes, Bousquet ski area, and the Ashuwillticook Rail Trail.
🗳️ CIVIC LIFE & POLITICS
  • 🏛️ Government: Mayor–Council form. The City Council has 11 members — 7 ward councilors + 4 at-large — meeting at City Hall, 70 Allen Street.
  • 👔 Mayor: Peter Marchetti, elected 2023 (check the Mayor's page for current office hours).
  • 🗺️ County seat of Berkshire County; part of MA's 1st congressional district — the westernmost turf in the Commonwealth.
  • 🗳️ Voting: register, find your ward/precinct, and see election dates via the Registrar of Voters.
  • 📣 Hot topics: the Housatonic River PCB cleanup, downtown revitalization, housing, and the eternal pothole question. Public comment is your friend!
