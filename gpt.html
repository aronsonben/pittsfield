01 / A city in the Berkshires
Pittsfield sits in western Massachusetts, in the Housatonic River valley, surrounded by the Berkshire landscape.
02 / Industry & reinvention
Manufacturing shaped the city's growth; today, local identity blends historic neighborhoods, arts, education and outdoor life.
03 / Culture close to home
The wider region is known for music, theater, museums, independent arts and summer performances at Tanglewood.
