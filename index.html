welcome to the pittsfield, ma ai contest!

date created & last updated: oct. 14, 2026

pittsfield, ma real life home page: pittsfieldma.gov

prompt used:

Using only HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, make me an 'early internet' webpage to act as the digital home for the town of Pittsfield, MA. It should provide links to all important town services, such as garbage pickup schedules and 311 hotline, but also have "digital homepage" type features like a forum board, a "town square" space where users can view in livetime the other users on the page, and should feature information on the town's history, culture and politics. It should be informed by the frutiger aero aesthetic and have a textured background, using images that have been dithered or can be manually dithered by the user on the page at the click of a button. Lastly, it should support multiple styles that can be chosen by the user. One might focus on blues and greens while the other could be gray and orange, so and so forth - you decide the best 3-5 styles to include based on what you know about Pittsfield, MA and early web aesthetics.

overall reflection:

it's really hard to get ai models to generate an artsy old internet web aesthetic. the models seem averse to anything too wacky or not-aligned.

that said, my prompt clearly had a big sway on the implementation. i mentioned 311 and trash pickup, they all put that front & center. some understood that i only meant those as examples.

kimi was the only one that i felt like got close to a frutiger aero aesthetic

most impressive feature-wise was the 20min claude (browser). interactive town square with image as background plus full-page background dithering on svg or user-uplodaed photo. impressed on that.

the chinese models were not great at understanding the purpose of the town website, i feel

all models can easily handle the tech restrictions and do a lot with them (dithering, town square, etc.). need to review code but good to know that they can do basically anything i throw at them with html, css + vanilal js.

simulating user experience: so funny that all models fill out fake human interaction as so wholesome ("who's seeing the show at __ tonight", etc.) instead of the absurdly hot takes and insane shit that happens on town facebook pages, etc.
